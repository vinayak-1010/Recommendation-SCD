# Product Vision & Non-Negotiables

**Project:** Explore Doon × Mussoorie (AWS Student Community Day 2026 · Graphic Era Deemed to be University)

---

## 1. Vision & Core Mission

Every participant of AWS Student Community Day at Graphic Era (Deemed to be University), Dehradun should be able to open one link, say *when they are free, how they travel, and what they enjoy*, and receive a plan that feels like it was written by a friendly local:
- Where to go and why
- What to do at each destination
- When and where to eat (spatially and temporally coherent)
- When to leave and exactly when they will be back at campus
- Sensible alternatives swappable in one tap

The product experience must feel **casual, smooth, and vibey** — a premium Apple-grade iOS-feeling experience wrapped in the AWS SBG GEU purple/dark brand tokens, never like a sterile questionnaire or form.

---

## 2. Priority Hierarchy (Higher ALWAYS Beats Lower)

1. **Feasibility & Safety:** Return to campus before the deadline, with realistic traffic buffer (P80 validation), and mountain descents completed before dark (sunset − 30/45 min).
2. **Geographic & Route Efficiency:** Zero pointless zig-zagging; no descending a mountain only to re-ascend or cross town needlessly.
3. **Relevance to Selected Interests:** High-affinity matches for what the visitor requested.
4. **Experience Diversity:** Balanced variety of activities without repetitive stops of identical experience types.
5. **Well-Timed Food & Café Opportunities:** Spatially adjacent meals within natural meal windows.
6. **Sensible Time Utilization:** Neither over-stuffed nor vacant.
7. **Alternatives & Flexibility:** Up to 2 coherent alternates per swappable stop with explicit trade-off deltas.

---

## 3. Product Non-Negotiables

- **Preferences, Not Quotas:** Selecting nature + views + adventure + food NEVER means forcing 4 separate nature stops and multiple restaurants. Three excellent, well-routed stops beat six rushed stops with heavy driving.
- **Deadline Strictness:** Reject any itinerary that breaks the return deadline, even if every stop matches user interests perfectly.
- **Alternatives Separation:** Alternatives are options for substitution, NOT additions to the active timeline or duration.
- **Zero Hallucinated Traffic:** Static estimates must never be masqueraded as real-time live traffic. Label conditions honestly as estimates based on typical conditions.
- **Honest Infeasibility:** When a time budget is too short for a distant hill destination, explicitly explain why and suggest the closest viable local alternative.
- **Deterministic Plans:** Same inputs and seed produce the same plan. Extending available time must never degrade objective score.
